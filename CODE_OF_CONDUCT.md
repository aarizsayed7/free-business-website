# Code of Conduct

This project is for people learning, helping and building together. Many contributors are students or first-timers, so be patient and kind.

**Expected**
- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, language or background.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain *why*, and suggest a fix.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.

**Not accepted**
- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information (including real customers' phone numbers or addresses).
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or low-effort pull requests made only to farm contributions.

**Enforcement**

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close pull requests, or block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
